8 FREE BLOCKY CHARACTERS - Rigged & Animated (CC0)
===================================================
Version 1.1. A free sample of the "48 Rigged Characters + 30 Animations" pack: 8 low-poly characters (hero, knight, wizard,
astronaut, pirate, robot, zombie, princess), each with the same 22-bone Mixamo-named skeleton and 10 animation clips,
one shared material and one 256x256 palette texture.

  Clips in this sample:  idle, walk, run, jump, fall, wave  (the classic six)
                         cheer, dance, punch, death         (4 of the 24 clips added in v1.1)
  The full pack has all 30 clips on all 48 characters (walk_back, strafe, sprint, crouch, kick, sword_slash, shoot, block,
  hit_react, clap, sit, interact, push, climb, swim ...).

  GLB/        8 x .glb   (web, Godot, Unreal, Blender)
  Unity/      8 x .fbx + palette textures + notes
  Godot/      small Godot 4 sample project (Up/Down cycle the clips)
  Textures/   blocky_palette.png (+ emissive mask)
  Preview/    cover and thumbnails
  LICENSE.txt CC0 (public domain): use for anything, no attribution needed

Specs: 1 unit = 1 m, about 1.8 m tall, origin at the feet, facing +Z, 2466-4628 triangles each.
Rigid skinning (blocky toy look), 30 fps clips, in place:
    Classic (v1.0)
        idle              2.0 s  loop
        walk              1.0 s  loop
        run               0.6 s  loop
        jump              1.0 s  one-shot
        fall              0.8 s  loop
        wave              1.6 s  loop
    Combat (new in v1.1)
        punch             0.9 s  one-shot
        death             2.0 s  one-shot
    Emotes and life (new in v1.1)
        cheer             1.0 s  loop
        dance             1.6 s  loop

Quick start for three.js / Unity / Godot / Blender: GLTFLoader + AnimationMixer for web, FBX with Rig = Humanoid or Generic in
Unity (see Unity/README_Unity.txt), open Godot/ as a project in Godot 4.
